How to use this book

  1. Think first. Attempt every "Think first" box and drill before opening the solution.
  2. Play. Every chapter has explorers — drag the sliders until the behavior is obvious to you.
  3. Drill to mastery. Tick a topic only when you can explain it aloud without notes and solved its drill. Ticks are saved in this browser.
  4. Come back. The review box below resurfaces what you mastered longest ago — re-test yourself cold.
  5. Build. Do the coding lab at the end of each chapter; implementation is what makes it stick.
